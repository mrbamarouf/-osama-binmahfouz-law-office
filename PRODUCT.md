# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Arabic-first and English-speaking visitors in Jeddah who need a clear way to understand the firm, review approved legal service areas, and contact the lawyer directly by phone, WhatsApp, email, or a front-end consultation form.

## Product Purpose

Present Osama bin Ahmed bin Salem bin Mahfouz as a lawyer and legal consultant in Jeddah through a premium bilingual website that communicates trust, clarity, confidentiality, and practical access without inventing credentials, metrics, teams, awards, client stories, or legal guarantees.

## Brand Personality

Luminous, measured, architectural. The brand should feel like a contemporary Jeddah legal atelier: calm daylight, refined olive, light limestone, precise typography, and direct contact paths.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic law-firm templates, dark navy legal branding, gavel imagery, handshake stock photos, foreign courtrooms, exaggerated gold, glass effects, huge empty hero sections, repetitive identical card grids, social links that do not exist, fake maps, fake statistics, and any mention of removed or unapproved services.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with trusted daylight: use bright architectural imagery and warm surfaces to feel premium without heaviness.
2. Make contact practical: every major flow should expose phone, WhatsApp, and email clearly.
3. Use editorial structure, not template grids: services, principles, and legal pages should have a designed rhythm.
4. Protect the facts: only approved information appears in content, SEO, structured data, and navigation.
5. Treat Arabic as primary: RTL, Arabic typography, and mobile readability are first-class.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-visible focus states, readable Arabic line lengths, reduced-motion alternatives, semantic landmarks, descriptive alt text, and no hover-only critical interactions.
